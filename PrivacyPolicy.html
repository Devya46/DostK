We may collect the following types of information:

Technical Information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide accounting, taxation, compliance, insurance, and financial services.
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests.

  • Improve website performance and user experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared only:

  • With authorized team members for service delivery.

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, or third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those websites separately.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
